# Furniture assets – licences

**Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)** – public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They were converted to self-contained binary glTF (.glb) with light optimisation
(dedup/prune/weld); geometry, textures and native units are unchanged.
The thumbnails in `thumbs/` were rendered from these models for this game.
